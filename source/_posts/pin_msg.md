Are you looking for resources to teach neurodivergent individuals life skills? Whether you are a parent or teacher, Flowers for Algernon provides such tools in many forms.

For every activity FFA hosts at our local special education school, we deliver it tin multiple digital forms to extend our impact. We produce:
- An activity guide for teachers
- An engaging video for kids
- An activity record

We hope as you explore our website, these can give you ideas of inspiration and that our experience can bring you insights.
